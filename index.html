I started at Heritage Bank dispatching cards, managing CMS issues and handling branch distribution across Experience Centres. What made that period formative wasn't just the operations. I was also part of the team that developed a new Card Management System. Most product leaders come into cards from the strategy side. I came in from the floor, and that changes how you think about what actually needs to work.

From there I moved into product properly, building debit and prepaid card programmes at Unity Bank, then scaling UBA's prepaid portfolio across 19 African countries. At Lotus Bank I led the acquisition of Visa Principal Membership and the Multipurpose Payment Card project, recognised at the Global Retail Banking Awards in Singapore. After that I crossed into fintech, heading up product at SID Digital where I built Snapii, Breeze, SecurePay and SIDios from the ground up.

I'm now Head of Cards at Kalabash Technology Solutions. I launched the Nigeria card programme from scratch, with Mastercard scheme membership, Wema Bank as BIN sponsor, Blusalt as processor, full app integration, three card variants including a premium product, and card issuance kiosks at Lagos airport. Next up is the Ghana Platinum Travel Card, launching November 2026.
